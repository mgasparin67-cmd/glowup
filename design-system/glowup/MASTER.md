# glowup visual system

Consumer facial proportions and skincare website. Approved direction: warm ivory, charcoal and coral derived from the supplied glowup logo. Editorial imagery and typography, with clear, practical form controls. Taste Skill guides the presentation; UI/UX Pro Max guides accessibility and responsive behavior. Dials: variance 6, motion 3, density 3.

The local UI/UX search matched a Minimalism & Swiss Style structure with editorial black and pink accents. Adapted to the owner's existing coral artwork and the approved warm editorial brief. The existing React/Vinext and native CSS stack is retained; no UI framework or animation dependency was added.

DM Serif Display headings, DM Sans body and controls. Fonts and their OFL licenses are served locally. Body text 16px, mobile inputs 16px, supporting text at least 12px. Numbers use tabular figures. Short transitions communicate hover and press feedback; reduced-motion disables transitions, animations and smooth scrolling. No scroll hijacking or perpetual decorative animation.

Header height 72–80px. Maximum page width 1280px. Asymmetric desktop hero with two-line heading, brief copy, one entry action and an editorial portrait. The generated portrait is captioned Editorial illustration; it is decorative artwork and has no connection to the analysis pipeline. Reserve its dimensions and serve it as WebP. Brand logo remains the exact extracted supplied artwork.

Photo upload and questionnaire retain their labels, limits, consent and cancellation behavior. Form controls have visible borders, focus outlines and minimum 44–50px touch areas; native checkboxes remain small inside full-size labels. Progress uses the same palette and the exported card uses the same fonts. Local fonts are loaded before canvas export.

Light theme throughout. Breakpoints at 480, 767 and 1024px. At the narrowest phone width, simplify header navigation rather than overflow. Stack photo and questionnaire below 768px. Keep descriptions and captions legible; do not add invented statistics, proof claims, analysis samples or a beauty score.
